Town Hall placement

Town Hall is 7×6 tiles. Drag it on the map, then use its lock button to keep that position during generation. Unlocked halls may be repositioned by the optimizer.

Expansion shape

Click any 4×4 expansion block to show or hide all 16 tiles. Dark blocks are unavailable to the optimizer. The grid is a planning canvas, not a claim that the city is rectangular.
